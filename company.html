Mission, Vision & Values

Building the future of prenatal genomic medicine.

Explore technology Meet our leadership
01

FGI will redefine prenatal care by engineering scalable and comprehensive non-invasive genomic technologies to deliver clinically relevant genetic information to patients.

Our mission

Building infrastructure

A new standard of prenatal care. Replacing fragmented testing with exome-level precision medicine.

Responsible translation

Translating decades of genomic expertise into improvements in fetal, newborn and pediatric health, without compromise or overstatement.

Leading the future

The future of diagnostics will be technology-driven. We intend to lead that future: ethically, comprehensively and transparently.

02

Our vision

Expanding the scope of prenatal genomics.

FGI aims to move beyond limited prenatal testing using gene panels, toward comprehensive clinical genomic sequencing.

Backed by decades of genomic medicine research, FGI operates at the intersection of biotechnology innovation and healthcare delivery, integrating sequencing technologies with expertise in fetal, pediatric, clinical and population genomics.

Exome-wide fetal sequencing

A first-of-its-kind non-invasive platform, designed to sequence ~23,000 fetal genes from maternal blood.
