FGI's Approach

Exome-level fetal screening.
A first-of-its-kind technology.

Fetal DNA circulates in a pregnant person’s blood. NIFS was developed to sequence this cell-free DNA to look at variation across the exome, the protein-coding regions of the genome.

Standard non-invasive prenatal testing (NIPT) looks at a limited number of common conditions. Thousands of genetic conditions fall outside that scope.2

Sequencing specimens from an invasive test can evaluate the whole exome, but the procedure carries a risk to the pregnancy.
