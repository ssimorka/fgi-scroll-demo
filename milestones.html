Genome-scale fetal sequencing has moved from feasibility to peer-reviewed validation. Prospective validation and launch preparation are now under way.

Overview

Eight years of research and clinical studies: a retrospective feasibility study, proof of principle in the third trimester, a prospective first- and second-trimester study, and large-scale validation published in NEJM. Supported by more than $10M in grants and internal funding to date, with initial launch planned for late 2027 / early 2028.
