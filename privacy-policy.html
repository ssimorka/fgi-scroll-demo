Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

First Genomic Insights, LLC ("FGI," "we," "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information through firstgenomicinsights.com and any related pages that link to this policy (the "Site").

This policy covers the Site only. It does not cover information collected in clinical studies, by our laboratory partners, or under separate agreements. Those activities are governed by their own consent forms and notices. FGI does not offer testing directly to patients or healthcare providers. If NIFS becomes available, independent laboratory partners will offer it. Those laboratories are responsible for any samples, test orders, results and related patient information, under their own privacy notices. FGI does not receive identifiable patient information from its laboratory partners. Please direct questions about a test or its results to the laboratory or to your healthcare provider.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form or email us, we collect:

  • Inquiry type (general, laboratory partnership, investor relations, research collaboration, media)
  • Name, organization, email address and, optionally, phone number
  • The content of your message
  • Information you send when applying for a job, such as your résumé and work history

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may collect:

  • IP address, browser type, device type and operating system
  • Pages viewed, referring URL, and date and time of visit
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information from cookies and similar technologies (see Section 4)

Health and genetic information. The Site does not offer testing, accept samples or provide results. We do not seek health, pregnancy or genetic information through the Site. Please do not submit it. If you do, we will use it only to respond to you and will delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and route them to the right team
  • Evaluate partnership, research, investor, media and employment inquiries
  • Operate, secure, maintain and improve the Site
  • Understand how visitors use the Site, in aggregate
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. How we share information

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers who host the Site, process form submissions, provide email or analytics, or support our operations. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and insurers.
  • Successors in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of FGI or others.
  • Others with your consent.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies, including analytics tools, to understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings and through our Cookie Settings link. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Site works.

Where the law requires consent (for example, in the EU, UK and Switzerland), we use non-essential cookies only after you agree. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out where required by law. We do not respond to Do Not Track signals.

5. Data retention

We keep contact-form submissions for 24 months after our last communication, unless a longer period is needed for an ongoing relationship or legal obligation. We keep job applications for 24 months. We keep analytics data in aggregated or de-identified form or delete it within 26 months.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know or access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Receive a portable copy of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising (we do not engage in these)

To make a request, email info@firstgenomicinsights.com. We will verify your identity before responding and will respond within the time the law requires. You may use an authorized agent where permitted. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

California residents. In the past 12 months we have collected identifiers, internet activity, professional information and inferences about general location, for the purposes in Section 2. We disclose these categories to service providers only. We have not sold or shared personal information.

Washington, Nevada and other consumer health data laws. We do not intend to collect consumer health data through the Site. If we receive any, we will not sell it and will use it only to respond to your request. You may ask us to delete it at any time.

EU, UK and Swiss residents. FGI is the controller of personal information collected through the Site. Our legal bases are legitimate interests (responding to inquiries, operating and securing the Site), consent (non-essential cookies), and legal obligation. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers

FGI is based in the United States, and we process information there. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for transfers from the EU, UK and Switzerland, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

The Site links to third-party sites such as LinkedIn and publishers of scientific articles. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post changes here and update the "Last Updated" date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice where required.

Regulatory status. NIFS (Non-Invasive Fetal Sequencing) is an investigational test under development and is not currently available for clinical use. It has not been cleared or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or any other regulatory authority. FGI does not offer testing directly to patients or healthcare providers. If NIFS becomes available, independent laboratory partners will offer it, subject to applicable laboratory and regulatory requirements in each market.

Medical disclaimer. Information on this website is for general informational purposes only and is not medical advice. NIFS is a screening technology: screening estimates risk and does not diagnose. FGI does not provide testing, results or medical advice to patients or healthcare providers. Questions about prenatal testing should go to a qualified healthcare provider or genetic counselor, or to the laboratory that offers the test.
