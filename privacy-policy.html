Privacy Policy

Introduction

First Genomic Insights, LLC (“Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is a genomic diagnostics company committed to protecting the privacy and security of individuals who visit our website and use our services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information, including personal and genomic data, through our website (“Site”) and related services.

This policy is intended to comply with applicable U.S. federal and state privacy laws, including the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), where applicable, and other relevant regulations governing health and genetic information.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website
  • Healthcare providers interacting with services provided by us or our licensed partners
  • Individuals whose information is submitted via the Site

This policy does not apply to protected health information (PHI) handled under separate agreements (e.g., Business Associate Agreements, Clinical Study Agreements), which is governed by HIPAA and related policies.

Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

We may collect personal information that identifies you, such as:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Professional credentials (for healthcare providers)
  • Billing or account information
  • Communications you send to us

B. Health and Genomic Information

If submitted through authorized channels, we may process:

  • Genetic test results
  • Biological sample data
  • Clinical or diagnostic information

Such data is handled in accordance with applicable healthcare privacy laws and contractual obligations.

C. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

How We Use Information

We use collected information for purposes including:

  • Providing and improving our diagnostic services
  • Communicating with healthcare providers and users
  • Processing transactions and managing accounts
  • Conducting research and development (in de-identified or aggregated form where possible)
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Maintaining website functionality and security

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal data based on:

  • Consent
  • Performance of a contract
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate business interests (e.g., improving services)

Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information with:

  • Healthcare providers and authorized partners
  • Laboratories and service providers supporting our operations
  • Regulatory authorities, when required by law
  • Third-party vendors (e.g., IT, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements

We do not sell personal or genetic information.

Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure data storage environments

Despite these measures, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations

Retention periods for genomic and health data may be longer due to clinical and regulatory requirements.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@firstgenomicinsights.com.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyze usage patterns
  • Enhance user experience

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

International Users

If you access our Site from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S., where privacy laws may differ.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
