A small team with a large remit: taking non-invasive fetal sequencing from peer-reviewed validation into clinical care.

Why FGI

Each sample is a family waiting on an answer. We are building the infrastructure for a new standard of prenatal care, and every role here, at the bench, on the platform or in the clinic, shapes what that standard becomes.

Work and life at FGI

What it is like to work here.

Purpose you can measure

Every study, assay and line of code moves a first-of-its-kind test closer to the clinic, and to the families it is for.

Founder-led science

Work directly with the physician-scientists who built the field, across Harvard, Columbia and the Broad Institute.

Small team, large remit

A company at the stage where one person can own a whole problem, from bench to validation to launch.

Rigor without overstatement

Peer-reviewed validation is the bar. We say what the data supports, and we design for the next question.

Bench, platform and clinic together

Sequencing, bioinformatics, clinical genetics and maternal-fetal medicine in one conversation.

Grow with the launch

Roles and responsibilities expand as the platform moves from validation into clinical delivery.
