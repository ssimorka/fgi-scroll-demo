How partnership works

From first conversation to NIFS on your bench.

  1. 01

    Initial conversation

    We learn about your laboratory's testing volume, patient population and current NIPT or prenatal offering.

  2. 02

    Technical and regulatory fit

    We review sequencing capacity, bioinformatics integration and the regulatory path in your market together.

  3. 03

    Validation and integration

    NIFS is validated on your instrumentation and integrated into your existing lab and reporting workflow.

  4. 04

    Go live

    Your laboratory begins offering NIFS to patients, backed by FGI training and ongoing technical support.

What partner laboratories need

What it takes to bring NIFS in-house.

  • Accreditation

    A CLIA-certified, CAP-accredited laboratory (or the regional equivalent) with an active prenatal testing menu.

  • Sequencing capacity

    Next-generation sequencing infrastructure capable of the depth and throughput NIFS requires.

  • Bioinformatics integration

    A pipeline and LIS able to integrate FGI's variant interpretation workflow and reporting.
