How partnership works

What partner laboratories need

What it takes to bring NIFS in-house.

Accreditation

A CLIA-certified, CAP-accredited laboratory (or the regional equivalent) with an active prenatal testing menu.

Sequencing capacity

Next-generation sequencing infrastructure capable of the depth and throughput NIFS requires.

Bioinformatics integration

A pipeline and LIS able to integrate FGI's variant interpretation workflow and reporting.
