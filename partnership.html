How partnership works

From first conversation to NIFS in your laboratory.

  • Together
  • FGI leads
  • Your laboratory leads
  1. 1

    Step 1, Together: Initial conversation

    We learn about your prenatal testing menu, sequencing capacity and patient population, and share an overview of NIFS and its supporting data.

  2. 2

    Step 2, Together: Technical fit

    We review sequencing infrastructure, bioinformatics integration and your prenatal testing workflow to confirm NIFS fits your laboratory.

  3. 3

    Step 3, FGI leads: Sublicense agreement

    FGI grants your laboratory a sublicense to use the NIFS technology, with terms covering field of use, territory and support.

  4. 4

    Step 4, FGI leads: Technology transfer

    SOPs, technical guides, a bioinformatics package and hands-on training for your laboratory and bioinformatics teams.

  5. 5

    Step 5, Your laboratory leads: Internal validation, designed by you

    NIFS is a laboratory developed test (LDT). You design and validate the assay under your quality system to meet local regulatory requirements. FGI provides technical guidance and support.

  6. 6

    Step 6, Your laboratory leads: Partner laboratory launches NIFS

    When validation is complete and approved under your quality system, NIFS can be added to your test menu.

What partner laboratories need

What it takes to bring NIFS in-house.

  • Accreditation

    A CLIA-certified, CAP-accredited laboratory (or the regional equivalent) with an active prenatal testing menu.

  • Sequencing capacity

    Next-generation sequencing infrastructure capable of the depth and throughput NIFS requires.

  • Bioinformatics integration

    A pipeline and LIS able to integrate FGI's variant interpretation workflow and reporting.

Training and support

Support from validation through the term of your agreement.

  • Onboarding and training

    Hands-on training for your laboratory and bioinformatics teams before validation begins.

  • Ongoing technical support

    Access to FGI's bioinformatics and technical teams during validation and for the term of the agreement.

  • Assay updates

    Access to assay and interpretation updates as the platform and the published evidence evolve.
